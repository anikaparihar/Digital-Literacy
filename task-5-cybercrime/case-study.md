## UPI/Online Payment Fraud

Riya, a college student, decided to sell her old textbooks online. Soon after posting the ad, a man called, sounding genuinely interested. He said he would buy the books and pay her using UPI.

To “send” the money, he asked Riya to scan a QR code and enter her UPI PIN. Since she had used QR codes for payments before, she didn’t suspect anything. However, within seconds, instead of receiving money, ₹5,000 was deducted from her account. Shocked, she tried calling the man back, but he made excuses and quickly disappeared.

This is a common UPI scam tactic today. Scammers pose as buyers or officials and trick victims into approving a payment request. They first build trust, then send a QR code or payment request and convince the victim to enter their UPI PIN, which results in money being debited instead of credited.

Such scams often target students, people new to digital payments, and small vendors. The consequences include financial loss, anxiety, and a lingering feeling of being misled.

Riya later realized that receiving money never requires entering a PIN or scanning a QR code. This incident highlights the importance of staying alert and carefully verifying any payment request, no matter how genuine it may seem.
