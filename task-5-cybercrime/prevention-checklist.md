## Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

- Use strong, unique passwords for every account (avoid using your name or DOB).
- Enable **two-factor authentication (2FA)** on important apps like email, banking, and social media.
- Never click on suspicious links or unknown attachments, even if they look “urgent.”
- Verify unknown calls/messages claiming to be from banks, colleges, or delivery services.
- **Do not share OTPs, PINs, or passwords** with anyone—no legitimate service will ask for them.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into sensitive accounts (banking, payments, etc.).
- **For UPI safety:** Double-check the receiver’s name before sending money.
- **For UPI safety:** Never approve “collect requests” from unknown people.
- Keep your apps and phone software updated to avoid security vulnerabilities.
- Limit personal information shared on social media (phone number, address, location).
- Log out from shared/public devices after use.
