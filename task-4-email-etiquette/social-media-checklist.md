# Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist

## ✅ Do’s
- Think before you post or comment  
- Keep your profiles professional and respectful  
- Verify information before sharing  
- Use privacy settings wisely  
- Be kind and respectful in interactions  

## ❌ Don’ts
- Don’t share personal or sensitive information  
- Don’t engage in cyberbullying or trolling  
- Don’t spread fake news or rumors  
- Don’t post inappropriate or offensive content  
- Don’t overshare about your daily life
